Kenya Grand Safari

The ultimate Kenya experience — Amboseli, Tsavo West, and the Maasai Mara in one spectacular journey.

Big Five GuaranteedMaasai MaraAmboseli ElephantsTsavo Red ElephantsLake Naivasha

Itinerary

1
Nairobi → Tsavo West
Drive to Tsavo West National Park, arriving in time for lunch. Evening game drive through volcanic landscapes and lava fields in search of the park's red elephants.
2
Tsavo West → Amboseli
Transfer to Amboseli, arriving for lunch. Evening game drive with classic elephant herds set against the Kilimanjaro backdrop.
3
Amboseli → Lake Naivasha
Early morning game drive in Amboseli before transferring to Lake Naivasha, with an afternoon boat ride on the lake.
4
Naivasha → Masai Mara
Transfer into the Mara ecosystem, arriving in time for an evening game drive across the reserve's predator-rich savanna.
5
Masai Mara Full Day
A full day in the Mara — the jewel of Kenya. Balloon safari available (extra cost).
6
Masai Mara → Nairobi
Final game drive before transferring back to Nairobi to conclude the Grand Safari.
